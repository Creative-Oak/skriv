# Spike 3 blind review: merge outcomes

Two people edited the same document at the same time without seeing each other's work. Then their copies were combined automatically. For each scenario there are three outcomes, **A**, **B** and **C**, produced by three different systems. Which system is which changes from scenario to scenario, and two outcomes may be identical.

For each outcome, please mark **acceptable** (what you would expect, or something you can live with without fixing it by hand) or **not acceptable**, and add a note if you like. Lines that differ from the starting document are marked ▶; lines that disappeared are listed as ✕ (removed). Bold and italic text are shown as `[b]…[/b]` and `[i]…[/i]`; sections are drawn with ┌ and └.

Generated by `cargo run -p skriv-crdt --example scenarios`. Do not open `key.txt` until all ratings are in.
